SEC+ OPS

A self-hosted, adaptive practice console for the CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) exam. Missing a question makes that topic surface more often until you've beaten it twice in a row — practice time goes where you actually need it.

  • Adaptive engine — every wrong answer raises that topic's selection weight (up to 15x baseline); two correct answers in a row decays it back down, so your weakest topics keep resurfacing without drowning out everything else.
  • 354 questions across 177 topics, plus 8 drag-and-drop performance-based questions appearing every 20th question.
  • Readiness report every 10 questions — overall accuracy, per-domain breakdown, and an accuracy trend chart.
  • Focus Mode, Review Missed, and full Exam Simulation — scoped practice, targeted remediation, and a timed no-feedback exam with scored history.
  • Progress is saved to your account automatically, so you can resume from any device.
